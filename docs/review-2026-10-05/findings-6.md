## Patterns

- **The Bab al-Mandab / Dhubab flood (09:28–12:04): about 22 duplicates.** A single government push (Dhubab town, then the district, airport, Al-Omari camp, Mayun siege, surrender call, flight by sea) became 40+ cards. This is the 3 Oct "hundred reports" mistake again. A rule that drops any card whose lead URL is already used, and folds same-place/same-claim cards within ~2h, would remove most of them.
- **Also lists folded by time, not by story: 8 also-wrong, plus 2 wrong-links and 5 more with bad Also.** Items posted in the same minute get attached with invented headlines.
