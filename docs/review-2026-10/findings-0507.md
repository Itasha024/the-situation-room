1,370 cards: 711 on 5 Oct (Operation Dawn of Yemen began), 520 on 6 Oct, 139 on 7 Oct to 09:40. Times are Israel time. Feed cards are left as they are. Map pins are corrected, and each new pattern became a rule (`docs/desk-lessons.md`, Stage 4b rows). Most of these cards came before the Stage 3 and 4a rules went live (7 Oct 09:15 and 09:41). ## 1. Wrong "←" source on front reports (a bug)

From 5 Oct 14:30 to 6 Oct 01:13, dozens of cards carried "←Marco Rubio": Saree's drone shoot-down, "Giants Brigades surround Mocha", "Three Saudi soldiers killed near the Yemeni border", a Houthi missile on a school. Others carried "←Esmaeil Baghaei" or "←US officials".

The desk holds a relay of a leader's words while it looks for the original. A second relay of the same words waits with it. The test for "the same words" was three shared words, and the Rubio relay's words were "Bab", "forces", "near", "Yemeni", "Saudi"… So 259 reports waited behind it and 49 behind Baghaei, each for up to 15 minutes, and many went out stamped with his name.

Fixed: the shared words now exclude the war's common words and front words. The report must also name the speaker, or else share five distinctive words. Of the 315 stored cases, 2 still match, and both are real Rubio relays.
