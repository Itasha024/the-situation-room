Desk header: variations of A3

A3 was a band of the flag between the title and the clock. Here it is again, now with Iran's flag drawn exactly. Under it are five variations. The side menu stays as it is.

What changed in Iran's flag. It is now drawn from the official design (the ISIRI 1 standard, as on Wikimedia), not by hand. It has three equal bands: green, white and red. The red emblem sits at the exact centre of the flag, in the white band. The takbir ("Allahu Akbar" in Kufic script) is written in white 22 times: 11 along the foot of the green band and 11 along the top of the red. In the header band, the emblem sits at the centre of the band, as it does in the flag. The takbir keeps its real size and spacing and repeats across the band's width.
